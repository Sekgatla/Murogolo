We learn together, we grow together.

Murogolo Primary School is a no-fee public school in Ha Muila, Kwaaidraai. From Grade R to Grade 7, our teachers and families work side by side to give every learner a steady start and a proud sense of home.

  • CAPS learning from Grade R to Grade 7
  • A no-fee public school for our community
  • Strong foundations in reading and numeracy
  • Parents, teachers, and learners pulling together

To the families of Ha Muila

Welcome to Murogolo Primary School. Our school is more than classrooms and a timetable — it is a place where children find their voice, learn to care for one another, and discover what they can do.

When the school and home stand together, our learners feel supported. Thank you for trusting us with your children and for the many quiet ways you help our school each day.

With care,
The Murogolo school team

Our Vision & Mission

The promise we make to children and families in Ha Muila

Our Vision
That every child who walks through our gates will be treated with care, encouraged to work hard, and proud of how far they have come.
Our Mission
To teach well, listen to our families, honour our community, and give learners the knowledge and confidence they need for the road ahead.
